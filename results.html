A handful of calculations is enough to train the model

We predict the lattice thermal conductivity of CoSb3 with a machine learning model trained on a small number of supercell calculations. The error in the forces drops quickly, and the conductivity settles from about eight supercells.

Dashed lines mark the conductivity obtained with the traditional approach using the LDA and PBE functionals. Experimental values reported for CoSb3 are around 10 W/(m·K).

Where p-type CoSb3 works best

The thermoelectric figure of merit, zT, depends on temperature and on how many charge carriers the material has. This map shows the calculated zT of p-type CoSb3. It peaks near 1.0 at high temperature and a carrier concentration around 2 × 1019 cm−3.

The values are read back from the colour scale of our original figure, so they are approximate. Point at the map or use the sliders.
